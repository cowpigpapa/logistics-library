## C. Internal guide material (cannot be checked against a public source)

Source: an anonymized internal Wet Damage prevention guide from one forwarder, November 2024 edition (drafts/wet-guide-anon.md). Every item below is labelled in the text as "한 포워더의 기준(예)" or "이 포워더의 지침". None is presented as law or as a CTU Code requirement.

- Three wet-damage causes: container defects, internal moisture and condensation, flooding in bad weather.
- Five-step description of condensation (one-line summary in 2절).
- Inspection timing (driver at pickup, packer before stuffing); day light test, night light test, water spray test. The forwarder receives the results.
- Inspection-sheet items. Sound container = 10 years old or less and passes the sheet. CY/depot supervisor and driver sign.
- Rejection criteria: age 13 years or more; dents that risk the cargo; light ingress; leaks; corrosion (rust comes off under a hammer, or 5 or more spots, or 20cm or more in total); vent covers; weld finish; ceiling welds over 1m total on 4 sides or 2 or more; wall welds over 50cm on the longest side or 3 or more; foreign matter on the floor that cannot be removed; odour; condensation is wiped, not rejected; gaskets; doors; missing trucker checklist = warning, 2 warnings = rejection. "4면 합계" for ceiling welds is the guide's wording, kept as is.
- Moisture-prevention packing: drying, avoiding wooden pallets, measuring humidity before and after stuffing, mandatory desiccants for high-value and water-sensitive cargo, NOR option, stronger wrapping, adjusting stack height, tarpaulin and pallets when an old container is unavoidable.
- NOR reduces outside-air ingress and inside/outside temperature differences: the guide's reasoning only. The public sources confirm the insulated structure, not this performance claim.
- Role split and bad-weather measures for each role (high-value cargo stacked on top; if stacked one high, placed where no flooding has occurred; drain checks; schedule changes; avoiding open storage).
- Air: BUP sheeting with 1 sheet underneath and 2 on top before netting; canopy storage; loading and unloading indoors when storms are forecast; sharing destination weather; wrapping and item-level plastic for electronics.
- Destination: receive results, share at once, Pre-Claim, collect evidence together with origin.
