## D. Maersk 할증료 용어집

- **Surcharge Definition Glossary (Maersk)** — https://www.maersk.com/solutions/shipping/glossaries/surcharge-definition (2026-10-18 내려받아 확인)
  - BAF: "The BAF surcharge accounts for the fluctuations in global bunker costs for 0.5% sulphur fuel."
  - LSS: "The LSS surcharge accounts for the additional costs associated with using low-sulphur fuel, which has a maximum sulphur content of 0.1%, for shipments that are transported entirely or partially through Emission Control Areas."
  - FFF: "This charge accounts for the fluctuations in global bunker costs for 0.5% sulphur fuel and the additional expenses associated with using low-sulphur fuel, which has a maximum sulphur content of 0.1%, for shipments that are transported entirely or partially through Emission Control Areas (ECA)."
  - EBS: "This fee is used in extraordinary circumstances, where unforeseen changes in bunker prices cannot be covered by the carriers standard Bunker Adjustment factor charges."
  - EMS: "The Emission Surcharge (EMS) will be implemented to recover the costs incurred by Maersk in complying with Emission Regulations. The surcharge will be applicable to all bookings on contracts with a validity of over one month. As this is mandated by regulation, it will be applied to all contracts, including those with an NNS clause."
  - ESS: "… This surcharge will be applied to all online bookings (SPOT/GO) and to bookings on contracts with a validity of less than one month."
  - PSS: "This is a seasonal fee applied during high volume shipping periods in certain trades. The fee is applicable to all shipments that move in these trades during the high volume periods."
  - ERS: "This fee covers additional costs faced by the carrier when moving cargo in dangerous regions including those that are threatened by hazards, violence or piracy. The charge covers extra bunker cost (due to longer routing and/or faster sailing), insurance coverage, and additional security measures. The fee will be applied to bookings that are from, to or transited through the affected areas."
  - GRS (Gulf Emergency Risk Surcharge): "This fee covers additional costs faced by the carrier when moving cargo in Gulf area due to increased tension. …"
  - ECS: "ECS stands for Emergency Contingency Surcharge … ECS covers the wider ripple effects like congestion, bottlenecks, missed sailings, equipment shortages etc"
  - TDS: "The Transit Disruption Surcharge (TDS) quantifies the amount of additional freight owed under the Maersk Bill of Lading when Maersk faces hindrance, risk, danger, delay, or any other disadvantage whatsoever which, in Maersk’s sole discretion, prevents the shipment from being routed via (i) the originally planned route, or (ii) a route which Maersk considers to be optimal and most efficient. … including those related to wider disruptions caused by the ongoing crisis in the Red Sea."
  - CFO/CFD: "This is a fee applicable when the carrier continues to offer services to a congested port, despite operational difficulties at Origin. The fee covers the additional vessel costs associated with delayed berthing windows."
  - LWC: "Charge applied to recover higher operation costs to be paid to inland operators due to low water levels in rivers."
  - PEO/PED: "It covers the extra cost at Origin, e.g. container moves in terminals, to position equipment into a certain deficit area or evacuate equipment out from a certain surplus area …"
  - Fixed Bunker Insurance: "This charge covers the cost exposure the carrier has when exceptional bunker/emission surcharge element is agreed with a customer during their contract period."
