# Illustration audit 1: CTU, ULD, Container, Temperature, Cargo Risk

Date: 2026-10-22. Read-only audit; nothing in the repo was changed.

Scope:
- Raster and SVG files in images/ctu, images/uld, images/cn, images/tc, images/rk (36 base images; the -w480/-w640/-w960 variants are resized copies and were checked only to confirm they exist).
- The 32 inline SVG diagrams in uld-docs and tc-docs. These were rendered locally with PyMuPDF, and their numbers were checked against the SVG source. Korean glyphs did not render, and hatch patterns showed as black fills; those gaps are renderer artifacts, not defects.

Method: I opened each image and compared it with the img alt, the figcaption and the surrounding doc text. For the Cubestow and doc SVGs, I recomputed every plotted value from the coordinates.

References: all 120 distinct /images/{ctu,uld,cn,tc,rk}/... paths referenced from ctu-library.js, *-docs, index.html, tools/*.html, library/ (prerender) and sitemap.xml exist on disk. **No broken src.**
