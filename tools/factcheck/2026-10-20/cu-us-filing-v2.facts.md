## ACE Export Reports — Census TradeSource Issue 33, Summer 2026, https://www.census.gov/foreign-trade/aes/tradesource_jul2026.pdf
- "To access export reports, you must first complete the U.S. Census Bureau's vetting process and receive authorization. ... Upon approval, you can generate reports and review export filing history directly through the ACE portal."
- "Users may generate three standard report types using their Employer Identification Number (EIN), the AES 201, AES 202 and AES 203"
- "The AES 201 is the Filer Transactions Report requested by the filer and shows all export transactions submitted by the filer. The AES 202 is the USPPI Transactions Report requested by the USPPI and shows all export transactions filed on behalf of the USPPI."
- "The AES 203 is the USPPI Agent Filed Routed Transactions Report and shows all routed transactions filed on behalf of the USPPI."
- "ACE Export Reports provides export data filed in the last 5 years, in addition to the current year."

## AMS cargo description
- 19 CFR 4.7a(c)(4)(vii) (eCFR 2026-10-16): "A precise description (or the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) numbers to the 6-digit level ...) ... Generic descriptions, specifically those such as 'FAK' ('freight of all kinds'), 'general cargo', and 'STC' ('said to contain') are not acceptable" (already in v1).
- 4.7(b)(2): manifest per vessel (already in v1 facts). The roll-over sentence ("새 선박 기준으로 다시 맞아야") is an inference from the per-vessel manifest rule, not a quoted CBP statement.
- CBP Publication #3714-0524 "Examples of Unacceptable vs Acceptable Cargo Descriptions", https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/acceptable-cargo-desc-508c-05.16_0.pdf: "A precise cargo description is a description of an item that is clear and concise. The description should be in plain language and detailed enough to allow U.S. Customs and Border Protection to identify the size, shape and characteristics of the commodity. ... Superfluous information ... e.g., personally identifiable information (PII), type of packaging, carrier disclaimers, etc., should not be transmitted"; "This list is not exhaustive and will continue to expand ... Descriptions in the Acceptable column should be viewed only as examples ... and not as a list of specifically acceptable or restrictive terms." Table rows used: Auto Parts -> Air Filters / Automobile Brakes; Machine Parts -> Oil Pumps / Silicone Seals; Spare Parts -> Cellphone Replacement Screen; Gifts -> Dolls, Basketball, Toy Car; Electronics/Electronic Goods -> Computers, Monitors, ... Mobile Telephones; Equipment -> Oil Well Equipment ... Compact Farm Tractor; brand names by themselves -> "Bubbles Brand" Laundry Detergent; Consolidated (on house or simple bills) -> only on master bills; STC, General Cargo, FAK, Various, Misc, Unknown, Freight Prepaid, B/L reference, Serial Number only -> clear and concise description required.
- CSMS #60011750 (2024-04-01), https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/393b4e6: "On April 1, 2024, CBP will nationally implement cargo messaging ... on shipments that have vague noncompliant cargo descriptions."; "including, but not limited to, 'gift,' 'daily necessities,' 'accessories,' 'parts,' and 'consolidated.' 'Consolidated' is only acceptable at the master bill level."; "Brokers and Freight Forwarders who self-file House Bills are held to the same standard as carriers".
